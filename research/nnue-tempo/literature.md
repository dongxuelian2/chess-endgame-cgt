# Prior work and experimental choices

The closest comparison is Pálsson and Björnsson’s IJCAI study. Its 100,000 sampled Leela positions, Stockfish 14.1 classical-term surrogates, input and first-hidden-layer probes, and qualitative disagreements motivate a different target here. Hold material and squares fixed, vary the turn, and ask an independent game-theoretic oracle which evaluation differences correspond to an outcome change. The exact classifications of mutual zugzwang already exist. The research question concerns the evaluators and their internal computations.

| Primary source | Result or design decision relevant to this work |
|---|---|
| [Pálsson & Björnsson, *Unveiling Concepts Learned by a World-Class Chess-Playing Agent*, §§4.1–4.4](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2023/0541.pdf) | The authors probe handcrafted concepts and identify fork, promotion and king-safety examples. We use the same release for the central comparison, follow the eight-unit bottleneck to the output, and score both evaluators against Syzygy. |
| [McGrath et al., *Acquisition of Chess Knowledge in AlphaZero*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.09259) | Layer and checkpoint comparisons reveal where human concepts become decodable. Their untrained-network control illustrates the importance of information already available in the inputs. Our algebra explicitly removes an entire input-to-output branch from the paired contrast. |
| [Schut et al., *Bridging the Human–AI Knowledge Gap*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16410) | Prototypes and human transfer experiments assess whether a concept can be taught. Here each proposed interpretation has an observable chess edit, with its own downstream score and exact outcome measurement. Human learnability remains a separate endpoint. |
| [Jenner et al., *Evidence of Learned Look-Ahead in a Chess-Playing Neural Network*, §§2.3–3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00877) | Patching Leela activations across carefully selected positions tests their causal role in move preferences. Our donor and recipient are legal placements with the same two kings, rook and pawn file. We patch a scalar value network and record target as well as output changes. |
| [Karvonen, *Emergent World Models and Latent Variable Estimation in Chess-Playing Language Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.15498) | Steering board- and skill-related representations changes token predictions in a sequence model. NNUE takes pieces and squares explicitly. The inference of interest here concerns the value of owning the move. |
| [Lin et al., *Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10158) | Sparse replacement layers and interventions trace tactics in Leela. The small dense Stockfish 14.1 head allows an exact integer replay of every downstream layer using the original weights. |
| [Ruoss et al., *Amortized Planning with Large-Scale Transformers*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04494) | Action-value distillation can produce strong decisions in a single forward pass. Good static scores can arise through supervised approximation. Our experiments test the score, the mechanism carrying one contrast, and the selected move separately. |
| [Haworth, *3–5 Man Mutual Zugzwangs in Chess*, §1](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/5950/1/2001g_CoW6_H_3-5-Man_Mutual_Zugzwangs_in_Chess.pdf) | Reciprocal zugzwangs were enumerated using independent endgame tables and divided into three outcome types. We use the existing outcome concept and enumerate the specified pawn domain to measure misses and false alarms. |
| [Bleicher & Haworth, *6-Man Chess and Zugzwangs*](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/4518/1/ACG12_6-man_Chess_and_Zugzwangs_v9.19.pdf) | Distinct depth and outcome metrics yield different kinds of zugzwang. Our labels use a strict WDL change at a fresh halfmove clock. Same-outcome changes of distance belong to another target. |
| [Hewitt & Liang, *Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks*](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/) | A high-capacity probe can learn the target itself. Our primary metrics use released weights, a threshold-free rank statistic, a simple explicit rule, and source-to-output readout checks. |
| [Elazar et al., *Amnesic Probing*](https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.10/) | Information removal tests whether downstream behavior depends on a representation. We intervene on every dense coordinate and evaluate discovery-selected coordinates on disjoint king configurations. |
| [Zhang & Nanda, *Towards Best Practices of Activation Patching*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042) | Metric and corruption choices can alter localization. We use both a constant clamp and a natural pawn-edit donor, record both patch directions, and compare early and near-promotion edits. |

Architecture, weights and table semantics are documented by the [Stockfish team](https://official-stockfish.github.io/docs/nnue-pytorch-wiki/docs/nnue.html), the [released network repository](https://github.com/official-stockfish/networks), and the [Syzygy probing implementation](https://github.com/niklasf/python-chess/blob/6e58bd46d99d40e6a75f2ab7b8f6e8c3f819ba2a/chess/syzygy.py). The review targets the intersection of these questions. Priority remains open.
