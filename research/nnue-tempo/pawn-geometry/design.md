## Pawn displacement transfer test

Discovery uses the 38 KRvKP classical collisions, including the 19 double successes. Freeze the following sampling and measurement plan before scoring fresh KRvKP, KQvKP, KBvKP, KNvKP, KRPvKP, KBPvKP and KNPvKP positions. The moving side is White. The black pawn displacement is one rank toward promotion, with both kings, the other men and the player fixed. Both white candidates move the same nonpawn, nonking piece quietly without check in both positions. Exclude terminal children. Sample new placements with a fixed seed per material and exclude the 38 discovery placements and their file reflections.

- Double diagonal. The pawn can legally take B only before the displacement and A only after it. A and B are the identical two moves in both contexts. The directional prediction favors A more before the edit.
- Single diagonal. B is uniquely exposed in the original context with neither candidate exposed after it, or A is uniquely exposed in the edited context with neither exposed before. Same directional prediction.
- Promotion. The pawn goes from rank three to rank two. Neither candidate lands on a square the pawn can capture. In the edited context B blocks a forward queen promotion or can legally take the newly promoted queen immediately; after A a forward queen promotion is legal and the moving piece cannot recapture it. The directional test asks whether B gains relative to A.
- Control. Neither candidate is legally capturable by the black pawn in either context, the pawn starts above rank three, and the two destinations agree on geometric control of its promotion square in the edited context. Assign the candidate orientation by chance.

At most one comparison per root and stratum. Cap each at 1,500 per material and stop after 90,000 candidate placements per material. Counts measure the designed samples. Primary outcomes are native score direction and WDL direction on all comparisons, and strict evaluator accuracy conditional on an actual WDL reversal. Ties are reported separately. Restrict one defender reply type for exactly one ply, then resume unrestricted Syzygy play, to measure whether pawn capture or promotion was necessary for the observed WDL reversal. Keep counts of cases with no alternative reply. For NNUE, remove the native input move-by-context interaction and rerun the head on an independently selected subset. Contrast KRvKP with each other family; both tasks share chess geometry but their trained heads and game outcomes may differ. Claims concern static SF14.1 and unlimited-clock WDL.
