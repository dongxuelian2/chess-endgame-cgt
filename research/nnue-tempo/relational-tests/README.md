# Which distinctions in an NNUE evaluation reach a chess decision?

We study Stockfish 14.1 with its native classical evaluator and frozen NNUE network. A score can distinguish two positions, survive three plies of adversarial choice, and improve the eventual tablebase outcome. These are separate measurements. The experiments use exact legal move generation and state-consistent alterations on finite search graphs. [Derivations and complexity bounds](decision-bounds.md)

## Information inside a classical tie

Order leaves first by classical score, then by NNUE score. Use this same lexicographic order at every maximum and minimum. The classical coordinate at every node is preserved exactly. When a classical root tie contains both wins and draws or losses, NNUE can therefore be given credit specifically for recovering chess information within a classical equality. Break remaining root ties uniformly. The outcome measure is the expected Syzygy sign under that tie break, using +1 for a White win, 0 for a draw and −1 for a loss.

| Data and classical baseline | Roots | Classical ties | Ties narrowed | Resolved ties spanning WDL outcomes | Within those ties, improved / equal / worse |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Discovery, released classical | 840 | 375 | 324 | 38 | 34 / 2 / 2 |
| Disjoint replication, full classical before grain rounding | 1,400 | 586 | 487 | 54 | 47 / 6 / 1 |

The replication disables both classical lazy cutoffs and retains the pre-grain integer score for general evaluation. Specialized endgame functions retain their native scores. In the 54 informative roots the mean gain was 0.301. In each of 255 controls we permuted NNUE scores among editable leaf states of *identical* classical value within each root, keeping transpositions consistent. This preserves the score histograms and allows arbitrary tie resolution to benefit from the structure of minimax. It yielded a mean gain of 0.123 and a central 95% range of 0.041–0.200. The one-sided Monte Carlo tail was 1/256. This conditional comparison measures placement of NNUE information among classical ties. In the full replication, 1,354 roots had complete WDL for the classical and refined choices. Their mean improvement was 0.012. The played and generated halves respectively had 20 and 27 improved roots, and one and zero worsened roots. Large effects in the informative subgroup and small effects over all roots describe different estimands. ### Which network computation carries the ordering?

The trained output sums a king-conditioned learned piece-square term (PSQT) and a positional head downstream of clipped accumulators, then truncates. Run the unchanged position through one native readout at a time and use its signed integer score as the secondary key. The table gives the mean gain over a uniformly broken classical tie on the same 54 roots. Each branch is scaled and truncated separately. Branch utilities describe interventions on decisions and can interact under minimax.

| Secondary score | Improved | Equal | Worse | Mean WDL gain |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native NNUE | 47 | 6 | 1 | 0.301 |
| Positional head | 46 | 6 | 2 | 0.282 |
| PSQT head | 29 | 6 | 19 | 0.004 |

Setting the secondary score to zero at checked and terminal leaves preserved the aggregate native result. On the 17 informative bishop-versus-pawn roots the positional branch improved all 17. On the 27 knight-versus-pawn roots it improved 24 and worsened one. [Readout substitutions](results/branches.csv) · [Paired per-root effects](results/mixed-roots.csv)

For a closer test, keep the kings and their feature addresses fixed. On four-man nonchecked Black-to-move leaves containing a black pawn and White minor, rook or queen, evaluate the positional output with all, either, or neither of the two non-king feature columns. Let the additive prediction be the sum of the two one-piece outputs minus the kings-only output. Its residual from the full score is an exact two-input mixed difference, including integer truncation. This choice of absent-feature reference fixes the decomposition. At other leaves retain the positional control, with zero secondary score for checked and terminal states. These interventions use internal feature masks. The original four-piece positions are legal.

Across the 54 roots the additive prediction yielded +0.149, the mixed difference substituted alone +0.180, and the intact positional branch +0.282. Zeroing the positional score on all designated four-man leaves gave +0.014. Conversely, using positional scores on just those leaves gave +0.268. In the knight subgroup, additive and mixed substitutions had the same aggregate +0.174 and the intact positional branch +0.304. The decomposition identifies joint dependence on the actor and pawn at fixed kings under an explicit anchor. Recovering a unique human concept from that dependence remains an open problem. ## A chess hypothesis for the bishop and knight results

Can the defending minor piece reach the black pawn's route before promotion? We fixed an empty-board rule and used 1,600 further generated root placements disjoint from both previous panels, 800 per material. Enumerate the pawn's straight advance to promotion, allowing its two-square start. For each route and each landing square, subtract the minor piece's empty-board move distance from the number of black moves taken to reach that square. Take the largest margin along the path and the smallest across possible paths. A larger margin favors White. The rule allows waiting and ignores check, kings, occupancy and captures. Unreachable squares receive distance 64. A square on a promotion move is included because White can immediately capture the promoted piece. Assign zero on leaves outside the nonchecked four-man test. Every variant uses the same full, pre-grain classical primary score.

Of 1,600 roots, 185 had classical best moves of different WDL. All 1,600 had tablebase coverage for the primary best set. The measure below is change in expected WDL when the secondary score resolves a classical tie, conditional on those 185 informative roots.

| Secondary score | Improved | Equal | Worse | Mean change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native NNUE | 176 | 5 | 4 | +0.345 |
| Minor-piece interception margin | 155 | 19 | 11 | +0.298 |
| Same calculation with the pawn's file reflected | 123 | 25 | 37 | +0.141 |
| Pawn rank alone | 51 | 88 | 46 | +0.043 |

Reflecting the pawn's file keeps the route lengths, rank and actor location fixed. Using its actual file increased utility by 0.157 over this control, with a descriptive paired 95% bootstrap interval of 0.116–0.201 clustered by 157 symmetry-reduced root king placements. The native advantage over the interception rule was 0.047 (0.011–0.083). The interception-selected set contained, on average, 74.6% of the NNUE-refined choices. A uniform classical tie break would land in that same set 38.1% of the time. This measures agreement on actions as well as agreement on outcomes. [Full results](results/interception.csv) · [Paired roots](results/interception-roots.csv) · [Intervals](results/interception-intervals.csv)

To directly compare readout preferences, find pairs of legal leaves within each of these roots with identical kings, pawn square, material, side, and full classical score. Require that Black cannot immediately capture the actor in either leaf. Keep actor-square substitutions where the actual-file and reflected-file margins give opposite strict orderings. Over 246,350 comparisons counted within roots, the native score favored the actual-file ordering 55.46% of the time, the positional branch 55.55%, and PSQT 50.00%, giving a tie half credit. The positional agreement was 58.64% for knights and 54.26% for bishops. This modest pair-level alignment and the stronger decision-level agreement quantify distinct properties. The geometric rule is a reproducible partial behavioral explanation. The strategic idea of reaching the pawn file ahead of the pawn appears in Fine’s classical analysis of knight endings. Exact internal computation of an empty-board race and identification of its strategic exceptions are open questions. ## What the rook formula discards

A discrete version of activation maximization enumerates both legal candidate moves and both pawn contexts. For 128 held-out symmetry-group representatives for king placement, four actor types produced 1,144,756 legal nonchecked child positions and 12,432,901 distinct common-source destination pairs across context blocks. Every admissible pair can arise from quiet, nonchecking moves of the same piece in both contexts. The two destinations form one unordered pair per block even with multiple possible sources. Optimize the signed four-corner contrast over both orderings. The frozen first-layer probe, its shuffled-coefficient control and the native score each have separate maximizing examples. The uniform baseline samples one ordered pair in each block. The probe's oriented maximum agrees with a change in pawn attack geometry in 58.1% of rook blocks, against 5.4% for uniform choice and 8.5% for its shuffled-coefficient control. For knights the corresponding rates were 20.7%, 5.5% and 6.7%. Readout maximization also finds high-scoring cases with absent or opposite geometry. [Population counts](results/maximization.csv) · [Maximizers and counterexamples](results/extrema.json)

In the strongest rook rectangle, White has Kh3 and Rd1, Black has Kc8 and a pawn on a3 or a2. The candidates are Rd3 and Rh1. From Black's perspective the classical function returned −1373 on all four children. With the pawn on a3 both children lose for Black. With it on a2 Black wins after Rd3 and loses after Rh1. The native NNUE scores in the same order were −1872, −679, +957 and −1635. Their alternating difference is 3785. Consequently every sum of an arbitrary rook-square score and an arbitrary pawn-square score at these fixed kings incurs at least 946.25 native units of maximum error in matching these four NNUE scores. Native units are the format of the evaluation trace. The tablebase demonstrates chess relevance independently of their scale. The specialized KRKP classical function consults the rook square only through one threshold on its distance from the defending king. The earlier audit identifies pawn captures and promotion interception on the original witnesses. Among 333 previously unexplained played-position move pairs, forbidding the immediate legal black king capture of the moved piece for exactly one reply erased or reversed the strict WDL preference in 155. There were 172 resolved cases retaining the preference and six unresolved. NNUE and classical favored the stronger choice in 82.3% and 65.8% of those 155, counting evaluator ties as half credit. Forbidding a nonpromoting pawn push erased or reversed the preference in 61 cases, with overlap possible. This experiment intervenes on the chess rules for one move. ## How many altered states can change the first move?

Use either a one-reply tactical extension or an internal feature edit. The initial 840 roots produced 1,472,720 three-ply leaf occurrences, representing 718,971 distinct states summed within roots. Of these roots 701 had a unique NNUE choice. For each Black-to-move leaf, the tactical alternative takes the lower White score of standing pat and the allowed responses. King responses capture a white nonpawn, nonking piece. Pawn responses capture such a piece or promote. Terminal values stay fixed. The minimum edit certificate counts each distinct state once, including transpositions that also occur under a rival root move.

Allowing both tactical classes, some subset could strictly displace the original choice in 150/701 roots. Of these, 108 admitted a single-state witness and the largest minimum was nine. For 42, activating the extension on every eligible state left the incumbent among the best choices. Using 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% of the full score change yielded 116, 132, 137 and 150 susceptible roots. In four roots, treating the rival as unaffected gave a different minimum. One of these had an apparent one-edit solution and was actually impossible. Among 148 feasible cases with tablebase labels for the selected incumbent and witness challenger, five improved, 133 stayed equal and ten worsened. This measures decision sensitivity to the specified extension. [Counts](results/certificates.csv) · [Example edit sets](results/witnesses.json) · [Independent optimality checks](results/audit.csv)

The signed internal edit targets states where a black pawn can legally capture exactly one identifiable white nonpawn, nonking piece, counting promotion choices with identical squares once. Write the raw feature vector as B+P+A for background, pawn and attacked actor. With C denoting clipping to [0,127], replace C(B+P+A) by C(C(B+P)+C(B+A)−C(B)), retaining the PSQT and all downstream weights. This projects away the anchored two-column interaction at the first clip within this support. It may move scores in either direction. Across the 701 unique-choice roots there were 5,679 increased and 9,938 decreased states, summed within roots. Only 12 roots admitted a displacing subset. Permuting these signed changes among eligible states within each root 255 times gave a mean of 16.66 and a central 95% range of 13–21. The primary upper-tail Monte Carlo probability was 255/256. The matching histogram controls the scale and number of perturbations. This narrow edit and the full positional-head decomposition use different supports, references and endpoints. ## Prior work and experimental scope

The IJCAI study already probes neural chess concepts and compares neural and hand-crafted evaluations. Its ECAI follow-up uses amnesic-like probes and self-play to measure behavioral relevance. Guid and colleagues derive human-readable strategies from endgame tablebases. DeepDream supplies the activation-maximization analogy. The chess-specific tests here are legal four-position extrema, comparator-preserving minimax refinement with tablebase outcomes, and state-consistent minimum intervention certificates with signed score changes. Each has an explicit counterexample or null control. The mathematical antecedents of approximation and minimax certificates are listed with the proofs. [IJCAI 2023](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2023/541) · [ECAI 2024](https://iris.landsbokasafn.is/en/publications/empirical-evaluation-of-concept-probing-for-game-playing-agents/) · [Tablebase strategy extraction](https://ailab.si/matej/doc/Deriving_Concepts_and_Strategies_from_Chess_Tablebases.pdf) · [Inceptionism](https://research.google/blog/inceptionism-going-deeper-into-neural-networks/) · [Derivations](decision-bounds.md)

All root panels are balanced selections from the earlier Lichess-derived and generated candidate pools. Discovery takes 60 and replication takes 100 distinct placements per source and material. Hash ordering is fixed before evaluation. The replication excludes exact placements from the discovery sample across all source groups. These pools condition on availability of eligible candidate moves in the original sampling. The complete search at each selected root includes every legal action, checks, promotions, captures and early terminals. Root history and castling rights are empty. Current side, piece placement and legal en-passant rights identify each state. There are no repetition or clock draws in this finite reference game. Mate receives ±30000 and stalemate or insufficient material receives zero. The leaf evaluations use the raw released SF14.1 NNUE, including on checked nonterminal leaves. Both the classical proxy and NNUE use that same boundary convention when the native classical function requires a position out of check. Reported native verification includes 620,073 and 1,009,746 nonchecked nonterminal leaf states, respectively, counted within roots. Syzygy labels collapse five WDL codes to their signs. Incomplete successor material coverage accounts for the per-comparison denominators. Transfer to adaptive production search, to other network releases and to tournament Elo requires separate evidence.

## Reproduction

Prepare the pinned network, the instrumented native binaries, Syzygy files and the played and generated source pools using the previous experiment. Install z3-solver for the independent budget oracle (tested with 5.4.1). From the repository root run the command below. The runner stores intermediary per-position data in scratch. Its exhaustive subset checks cover 3,000 random lowering games and 5,000 signed games. The rational strength checks include threshold ties. The independent SMT encoding checks feasibility at the stated minimum and impossibility below it, or impossibility with free budget, in 19 chess cases. The four strongest rectangles also receive independent legal-move enumeration and native score replay. [Preparation](../pawn-geometry/README.md)
